The publishing pipeline, end to end

The tool runs on the owner's own computer. It combines a content-preparation stage (done in advance, with human review) and a publishing stage (via TikTok's official APIs). Here is the complete flow:

1

One-time authorization (Login Kit)

The owner opens the TikTok authorization page, signs in to his own account @.doannguyenpro and grants the scopes user.info.basic, video.upload and video.publish. The tool stores the resulting token locally on the owner's computer only. Authorization can be revoked anytime in TikTok settings.

2

Content creation & review

The owner records and edits original 50–90 second science videos (vertical 9:16). For each video he prepares and reviews a caption: one hook line plus relevant hashtags. Nothing is generated or posted without the owner having seen it.

3

Automatic preparation

Before upload, each video is validated (format, orientation, duration) and re-encoded to H.264/AAC at TikTok-friendly bitrate and size. A local ledger records every published file so no video is ever posted twice.

4

Account confirmation

At posting time the tool calls the Creator Info endpoint to display the account's nickname and privacy options, confirming it is posting to the correct, authorized profile with the right privacy level.

5

Direct post (Content Posting API)

The tool initializes the upload (video/init), transfers the file in a single chunk, and TikTok processes and publishes it to the owner's profile with the prepared caption. In draft mode (video.upload), the video instead lands in the owner's TikTok inbox for final editing in the app.

6

Status check & logging

The tool polls the publish status until TikTok confirms success, then writes the result to a daily log. If anything fails, the video stays in the queue and the owner is notified.

Design principles

Human in the loop

Captions and videos are prepared and approved by the owner before any automated step touches them.

Official APIs only

All communication goes through developers.tiktok.com endpoints. No web automation, no scraping, no unofficial clients.

Minimal data

Only the owner's open id, display name and avatar are read — solely to confirm the right account. See the Privacy Policy.
