Frequently asked questions

Who is this tool for?

For one person: the owner of the TikTok channel @.doannguyenpro. It is a personal, single-user publishing pipeline. It is not offered as a service, has no sign-up, and never posts on behalf of anyone else.

Does it post content automatically without review?

No. Every video is recorded and edited by the owner, and every caption is prepared and reviewed by the owner before it enters the publishing queue. The automation only handles the mechanical part — encoding, uploading and publishing at scheduled times.

Which TikTok products does it use?

Login Kit (scope user.info.basic) for one-time OAuth authorization, and the Content Posting API (video.publish for direct posting, video.upload for draft mode). All calls go to TikTok's official endpoints — no browser automation, no unofficial APIs.

What data does it store?

Only the OAuth tokens and the owner's basic profile info (open id, display name, avatar), stored locally on the owner's own computer. Nothing is shared with third parties. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Can the authorization be revoked?

Yes, at any time from the TikTok app: Settings → Security → Apps and websites. After revocation the tool cannot access the account until the owner authorizes it again.

What kind of content is published?

Original short science and astronomy videos in Vietnamese — topics like neutron stars, black holes, gravitational waves and Earth science. Content follows TikTok's Community Guidelines; see Videos for examples.

Does it interact with other users (likes, comments, follows)?

No. The tool has exactly one capability: publishing the owner's own videos to the owner's own profile. It performs no engagement automation of any kind.

How often does it post?

Twice a day at fixed times (morning and afternoon, Vietnam time), matching the channel's regular schedule. A local ledger guarantees each video is published at most once.
