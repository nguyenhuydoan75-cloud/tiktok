Doan Nguyen Auto Post is the publishing tool behind @.doannguyenpro — a Vietnamese science & astronomy channel. It schedules and posts the owner's own short videos to his own TikTok profile through TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API. Nothing more, nothing less.

What this tool does

🔐

Official authorization

The channel owner signs in with TikTok Login Kit (OAuth). The tool never sees a password and access can be revoked from TikTok settings at any time.

🎬

Owner's content only

Every video is created by the channel owner — original Vietnamese narrations about space, physics and Earth science. The tool posts only to the owner's own profile.

🗓️

Consistent schedule

Two uploads per day at fixed times. The tool picks the next unpublished video from the owner's library, attaches the prepared caption and hashtags, and posts it.

✅

Review before publish

Captions and videos are prepared and reviewed by the owner in advance. A ledger guarantees the same video is never posted twice.

📦

Smart preparation

Videos are checked for 9:16 vertical format and compressed to TikTok-friendly size before upload, keeping quality while staying within API limits.

🚫

What it does NOT do

No posting for third parties, no scraping, no engagement automation, no comments/likes/follows. It is a single-user publishing pipeline.

About the channel

@.doannguyenpro — science for everyone

Short, carefully researched videos in Vietnamese about astronomy and physics: black holes, neutron stars, gravitational waves, the Casimir effect, interstellar visitors like ʻOumuamua, and what would happen to Earth in extreme scenarios. Each video is 50–90 seconds, vertical 9:16, narrated and edited by the owner.

Made by a teacher

The channel is run by Nguyễn Huy Đoàn, a secondary-school teacher in Vietnam who turns classroom questions into bite-size science stories. The same videos are also published on the owner's YouTube Shorts channel.

Integration at a glance

TikTok productPurpose in this tool
Login Kit (user.info.basic)One-time OAuth authorization by the owner; display name and avatar are shown so the owner can confirm the right account before anything is posted.
Content Posting API (video.publish)Direct-posts a prepared, owner-approved video with its caption to the owner's own profile.
Content Posting API (video.upload)Fallback mode: uploads a video as a draft so the owner can finish publishing inside the TikTok app.
