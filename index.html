Doan Nguyen Auto Post

Doan Nguyen Auto Post is a personal, single-user tool that helps its owner automatically publish his own short educational and science videos to his own TikTok account @.doannguyenpro, using TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API.

What it does

  • Lets the owner authorize his own TikTok account via official OAuth.
  • Uploads an MP4 video from the owner's computer.
  • Publishes it as a Direct Post with a caption and hashtags.

It is used only by the owner, for the owner's own content. It does not serve third parties.
