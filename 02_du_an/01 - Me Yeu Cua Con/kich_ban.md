## Suno
Title: Mẹ Yêu Của Con
Style: Vietnamese emotional ballad, female vocal, warm and tender, soft acoustic guitar, light piano, gentle strings, slow tempo 70 bpm, short song about 55 seconds
Exclude: rap, electronic, drums heavy, autotune, english lyrics, long intro
