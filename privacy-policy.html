Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Doan Nguyen Auto Post (the "App") handles data. The App is a private, single-user tool used by its owner to publish his own videos to his own TikTok account @.doannguyenpro via the TikTok Content Posting API.

1. Data We Access

When the account owner authorizes the App through TikTok Login Kit, the App receives an access token and the basic profile information (open id, display name, avatar) needed to confirm the correct account before posting. The App requests the scopes user.info.basic, video.upload, and video.publish.

2. How We Use Data

The access token and profile information are used solely to upload and publish the owner's own videos to his own TikTok profile. We do not use the data for advertising, profiling, or any unrelated purpose.

3. Data Sharing

The App does not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties. Data is exchanged only between the App and TikTok's official APIs.

4. Data Storage and Retention

Access tokens are stored locally on the owner's own computer and are used only to perform posting. Tokens can be revoked at any time from the TikTok account settings, after which the App can no longer access the account.

5. Third Parties

The App relies on TikTok's APIs. TikTok's handling of data is governed by TikTok's own Privacy Policy. The App is not affiliated with or endorsed by TikTok / ByteDance.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone under the applicable age of digital consent.

7. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this URL.
